Each dot is a card name leaf from the v4 model. Position is a UMAP of what training changed in its 256-d embedding. Cards with the same type and color land near each other, though the model was never told either.

Highlight up to three values at a time; everything else stays grey. Hover or search a card to see its five nearest neighbours by cosine similarity.

Type separates cleanly

A card's five nearest neighbours share its main type 71% of the time, against 48% for always guessing creature. Instants and sorceries pool together, away from creatures and lands.

Color comes through too

Neighbours share a card's color 51% of the time, against a 20% base rate. No cost or color leaf exists in the encoding, so this is learned from how cards are used.

Functional twins find each other

Llanowar Elves sits next to Fyndhorn Elves (0.76 cosine) and Elvish Mystic. Faerie Seer and Faerie Miscreant are each other's closest card.

Mostly still the starting point

Training moved each vector a median of 6% from its initial value. The raw embeddings cluster no better than chance, so the map uses the learned change.
